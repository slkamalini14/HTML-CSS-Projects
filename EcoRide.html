About EcoRide

EcoRide is on a mission to make everyday travel simple, affordable, planet-friendly. We provide easy bicycle rentals for commuters, students, and explorers who want to skip the traffic, cut down pollution, and stay fit - all while discovering the city on two wheels. Founded on the belief that smal changes create big impact, EcoRide is more than a rental service; it's a movement towards cleaner, greener transportation for everyone.


🗝️ Key benefits

  • 💪 Healthier lifestyle - Cycling keeps you active, improves fitness.
  • 🌱 🌱Eco-Friendly travel - Zero emissions, zero pollution. Every ride helps reduce your carbon footprint.
  • 💰 Affordable and Convenient - Budget friendly rental plans with flexible hourly, dialy, and monthly options.
  • 🏙️ Explore the city - Skip traffic jams and explore hidden spots, parks and shortcuts only accessible by two wheelers.
  • 📱 Easy booking process - Quick and hassle-free registration, rental and return - all in a few clicks.
